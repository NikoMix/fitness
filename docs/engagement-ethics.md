# Engagement ethics

Forge engagement features must support training without pressure.

- No manufactured loss aversion, guilt-inducing copy, artificial scarcity, or countdown pressure.
- Gamification must be fully disableable. Turning off badges, milestones, or streak surfaces must not change core workout logging, planning, nutrition, progress, backup, or settings.
- Streaks are forgiving: planned rest does not break a streak, freezes protect real-life interruptions, and recovery copy is encouraging rather than punitive.
- Achievement sharing is local-only: cards are rendered on the device and shared through the OS share sheet. Forge uploads nothing because v1 has no backend.

These rules are encoded in `EngagementEthicsPolicy` and covered by domain tests.
