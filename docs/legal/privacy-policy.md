# Forge Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-20

Forge is a local-first fitness app for Android and iOS. Version 1 has no Forge-operated backend, account server or cloud database. Your workouts, body metrics, nutrition logs, goals, health-platform imports and preferences are stored on your device.

## Health data stays on your device

Health and fitness data can reveal sensitive information about your body and routine. Forge does not sell health data, does not use health data for advertising and does not send health data to Forge servers.

If you choose to export a backup, share a diagnostic file or move a file with your operating system's share sheet, you control where that file goes. Once you share or store an exported file outside Forge, that destination's privacy and security rules apply.

## Data Forge stores locally

Forge may store the following categories on your device:

- workout plans, exercise history, sets, reps, loads and notes;
- nutrition, hydration and body metric entries;
- goals, preferences, units, notification settings and app-lock state;
- imported Health Connect or HealthKit values you explicitly allow;
- cached media and temporary export files needed for app features.

Forge stores app data in an encrypted local SQLite database. The database encryption key is kept in platform secure storage. Some non-sensitive preferences are stored through the platform preferences system.

## Permissions

Forge asks for permissions only when a feature needs them. Examples include Health Connect or HealthKit access, notifications, camera or file access. You can revoke platform permissions in Android or iOS settings.

## Advertising and analytics

Forge v1 does not use health data for advertising and does not include a Forge-operated analytics or crash-reporting service that phones home. Diagnostics are shared only by explicit user action.

## Backups and deletion

Because Forge has no cloud backup, the device is the system of record. If you uninstall Forge, lose your device or delete data without an exported backup, Forge cannot recover it.

The in-app **Delete my data** flow is available without contacting support. It is designed to erase:

- the encrypted local database;
- the encryption key in secure storage;
- cached media;
- preferences; and
- temporary export files.

Deletion is irreversible unless you exported and retained your own backup first.

## Children

Forge is intended for general fitness use and is not directed at children. Store listing age ratings and regional requirements must be reviewed before submission.

## Changes

This policy may be updated with future app releases. The published public URL should always match the current in-app privacy policy.
