## Not medical advice

Forge provides exercise, nutrition and habit guidance for general fitness. It is not medical
advice, diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional. No content in
Forge creates a clinician-patient relationship, and Forge cannot assess your individual health.

## Before starting

Consult a physician, physiotherapist, dietitian or other qualified professional before starting a
new programme, increasing training intensity, materially changing your diet, or using Forge after a
period of inactivity.

## Pain or warning symptoms

Stop exercising immediately if you experience chest pain, faintness, severe shortness of breath, an
unusual heart rhythm, sharp joint pain, sudden weakness, dizziness or any symptom that feels
unsafe. Seek urgent medical care when symptoms are severe or persistent.

Forge cannot detect a medical emergency and must never be relied on to do so. In an emergency,
contact your local emergency services.

## Pregnancy

If you are pregnant, recently gave birth or are trying to conceive, get professional guidance
before following exercise or nutrition recommendations. Training intensity, hydration, calorie
targets and movement selection may all need individual adjustment.

## Cardiac conditions

If you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, a history of cardiac events, an implanted
cardiac device, or have been advised to limit exertion, use Forge only with professional clearance,
and follow your clinician's limits over any suggestion the app makes.

## Injury and rehabilitation

If you are injured, recovering from surgery or managing chronic pain, avoid movements that
aggravate symptoms and follow your clinician or therapist's rehabilitation plan. Forge cannot
assess tissue healing or judge safe loading for you.

## Nutrition and hydration targets

Calorie, macronutrient and hydration figures in Forge are general estimates from standard formulas.
They are not clinical prescriptions and do not account for medication, pregnancy, eating disorders,
diabetes, kidney or liver conditions, or other individual factors. Seek professional advice before
acting on them if any of these apply to you.

## Accuracy of imported and calculated data

Values imported from Health Connect, HealthKit or another app's export are only as accurate as the
device or app that produced them. Estimates such as calories burned are approximations, not
measurements, and should not be used for any clinical purpose.

## Your responsibility

You use Forge at your own risk, and you are responsible for training within your own limits. If any
guidance conflicts with advice from your healthcare professional, follow your healthcare
professional.
