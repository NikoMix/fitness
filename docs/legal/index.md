## Forge keeps your data on your device

Forge is a fitness app for Android and iOS. It has no Forge-operated backend, no account server
and no cloud database. Your workouts, body metrics, nutrition logs, goals and health-platform
imports are stored in an encrypted database on your own device.

This site publishes the same legal documents that ship inside the app, so you can read them
before you install anything.

## The short version

- Forge does not operate a server that receives your health data.
- Forge does not sell your data, and does not use health data for advertising.
- Forge does not include analytics or crash reporting that phones home.
- Forge cannot recover your data, because Forge never has a copy of it.
