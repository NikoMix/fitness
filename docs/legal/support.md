## What to include

Support cannot see your device, and Forge has no server-side logs to inspect. A useful report
usually contains:

- the app version, shown in Settings;
- your device model and operating system version;
- what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
- the steps that reproduce the problem.

Do not email screenshots or exports containing health information unless support explicitly asks
for them. If you do send them, email is the least private channel involved, because your data has
otherwise never left your device.

## Sharing diagnostics

Forge can produce a diagnostic file on request. Diagnostics are created on your device and are
shared only when you choose to share them through your operating system's share sheet. Forge does
not upload diagnostics automatically.

## What support cannot do

Because Forge has no backend, support has no way to:

- recover data from a lost, reset or stolen device;
- restore data after an uninstall when no backup was exported;
- read, change or delete anything stored on your device;
- look up your purchase history, which belongs to Apple or Google.

If you lose access to a device without an exported backup, the data is gone. This is a direct
consequence of Forge never holding a copy. Export a backup regularly.

## Purchases, refunds and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play, not by Forge. Refunds, payment methods,
receipts and subscription management are handled by the store that took the payment.

- Apple: manage purchases and request refunds through your Apple account.
- Google: manage purchases and request refunds through your Google Play account.

Forge cannot issue a refund on the store's behalf.

## Privacy and data requests

For anything involving your data, start with the [privacy policy](../privacy/) and the
[delete my data](../delete-my-data/) page. Most requests, including erasure, are things you can
carry out yourself on the device without contacting anyone.
